Counting seconds in binary-coded decimal: one digit in each half of the byte
$09  "09"00001001nine seconds: high half 0, low half 9
+1 → $0A00001010a plain add makes $A in the low half: not a decimal digit
+6 → $10  "10"00010000skip the six codes $A–$F: the low half wraps to 0 and carries 1 into the high half
$59 +1 +6 → $600110000059 seconds and one more is 60, the limit for seconds…
→ $00, carry00000000…so back to 00, and the minutes byte gets the same +1
To print a byte, the high half is digits >> 4 and the low half is digits & 0x0F: add each to '0' and it's a letter. No dividing by ten.
