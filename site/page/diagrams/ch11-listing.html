01 0183 02BLOCKS FREE.00643 free: no quotes, so no castle

Each line: two link bytes, the line number low byte first, the text, a 0.
2. The names, less their Z, into the castle list at $B900
$B900TRAINING $B910ENTRANCE HALL
16 bytes a name. The highlighted letter has bit 7 set: the name ends there.
